Legal Compliance Rebuild

Maxim Autos legal pages: privacy, terms, return

Surgical compliance revision of three already-strong pages. Every drafted change was red-teamed, and every critical and high finding is now closed on the pages themselves.

Verdict: All three legal pages are compliance-clean and publish-ready. The one remaining risk is not on these pages: a Spanish marketing strip elsewhere on the site still advertises a "3 month warranty" that does not exist. That must be softened before or right after publish, and an attorney should review before go-live.

Top defects fixed, per page

Privacy Policy was critical

src/pages/privacy-policy.astro
  • Killed the false "no cookies, analytics, or pixels" claim. Replaced with a truthful Google Analytics 4 disclosure (ID G-H05CD3EHE9), the GA cookies, the generate_lead events, and opt-out choices. This was the number one fix.
  • Disclosed the real credit-app pipeline: Vercel Blob (encrypted storage) and Resend (alert email) as processors of financial data, plus Formspree for the other forms.
  • Effective date moved to July 12, 2026 and the 30 vs 45 day response window reconciled to 45 days.
  • Fixed wrong agency: "IDOT" corrected to Illinois Secretary of State dealer regulations.
  • Reframed Illinois "rights" as a voluntary courtesy, not statutory rights wrongly attributed to ICFA / PIPA / BIPA.
  • Disclosed Google Fonts, noted credit data can enter DealerCenter, softened the SMS STOP language, and named Maxim Autos LLC as controller.

Terms of Use was medium

src/pages/terms.astro
  • Added the 815 ILCS 505/2L carve-out to the blanket implied-warranty disclaimer, so the box no longer overstates that all implied warranties are disclaimed during the 15 day / 500 mile statutory window.
  • Corrected the Illinois Attorney General consumer fraud hotline to the Chicago line 1-800-386-5438 (right regional number for a Skokie dealer).
  • Standardized "AS IS" (unhyphenated, FTC-correct) and fixed copy-rule hyphens: "Third Party Links", "Buyers who live outside Illinois", "free of errors".
  • Named Maxim Autos LLC in the intro and contact block; effective date already July 12, 2026.

Return Policy was medium

src/pages/return-policy.astro
  • Corrected the statutory deductible. The old "a deductible per repair" implied an uncapped, every-repair charge. Now: one half of the cost of each of the first two covered repairs, up to $100 per repair, per 815 ILCS 505/2L.
  • Fixed the FTC box em dash to a hyphen to match the official Buyer's Guide punctuation. (Text itself is the complete current FTC box; see note below.)
  • Added Maxim Autos LLC and the Class D dealer license DL7667 to the contact block for enforceability.

Locked decisions honored

● Publish-ready

These are strong pages made accurate, not regenerated. Correct legal language (GLBA notice, statute citations, FTC box) preserved verbatim.

● AS IS default

All sales final, AS IS. No cooling-off period in Illinois. The only unwind path is the spot-delivery financing contingency.

● Statutory floor is the warranty

The 15 day / 500 mile 2L powertrain protection is the "peace of mind." No free Maxim warranty, no CPO, the word "certified" is never used as a claim.

● Copy rules

No em dashes, no hyphenated compounds in prose. Exceptions kept only for the FTC box and formal statute names. Voice is "we", warm and direct.

Open items for the human

1. Attorney review recommended before go-live

These pages are now factually accurate and internally consistent, but a licensed Illinois attorney should give a final read before publish. The content covers GLBA, ICFA, CCPA/CPRA, FTC disclosures, and the 2L statutory warranty, which is the kind of surface where a lawyer's sign-off is worth it.

2. Sitewide "free 3 month warranty" wording still needs softening (out of this scope)

The Spanish trust strip in src/layouts/Layout.astro renders Inspeccion · Garantia 3 Meses · CARFAX, and src/pages/es/index.astro shows Garantia de 3 meses. Both advertise a free three month warranty that does not exist under the locked stance and contradict the very return and terms pages under review. This is a deceptive-warranty exposure under ICFA and FTC Act Section 5. Suggested fix, mirroring the clean English strip: Inspeccion · CARFAX Gratis · Sin Cargos de Dealer. These files were outside the three-legal-page scope, so they were intentionally not edited here.

3. One red-team finding was verified rather than applied

The return page FTC "As Is" box was flagged as a truncated verbatim quote. Verification against the current FTC Buyer's Guide (revised 2018, 16 CFR Part 455) confirmed the box text on the page is the complete, current wording. The red-team was comparing against the pre-2018 version. Adding those old sentences would have made the box wrong, so only the em dash was corrected to a hyphen.

4. Ops note, not a page change

The credit-app alert email currently sends from a shared Resend sandbox sender. Moving to a verified maximautos.com sending domain would help deliverability and brand trust. No policy wording change needed.
