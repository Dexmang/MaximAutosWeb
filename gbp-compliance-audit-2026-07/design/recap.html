Honesty box

The website is not the reinstatement lever

The website did not cause the suspension. Forensics confirmed the cause was the 7/1 GBP badge-graphic posts, with delayed batch moderation explaining the 7/11 timing. This re-audit hardens the legitimacy signal and removes real FTC and structured-data exposure, but no fix here gets the profile back.

The reinstatement path stays the appeal already filed, verdict expected around Jul 17 to 18, plus purging the 7/1 badge posts right after reinstatement. Do not present any site change as the thing that restores the listing.

Because the site is not the lever, nothing here is a reinstatement emergency. Fixes are sequenced on their own risk merits: additive disclosures now, public review-representation changes after the verdict.

Phased roadmap

Phase 1

Safe during the appeal freeze

Additive disclosure and qualifier hardening, website only. Zero downside, ship anytime. Nothing here changes a public business representation.

  1. A2-01 Add the Reg Z footnote after the VDP More Vehicles grid. Closes the last F1 gap.
  2. A5-F1 Add qualifying to the Volvo VDP description and fix the DealerCenter template so it cannot recur.
  3. A3-01 Reword the after-repossession FAQ schema to qualifying units.
  4. A3-02 Add on qualifying cars to the Des Plaines suburb FAQ.
  5. A4-08 Align the contact page geo coordinates to the canonical pair.
  6. GL-01 Add the DL7667 license line to the sitewide footer. Legitimacy signal, currently buried in legal pages only.
  7. GL-02 Add the full streetAddress to the four incomplete PostalAddress schema nodes.
Phase 2

Gated to after the verdict

Anything that changes the visible rating, count, source, or any NAP or hours substance. These are signals a reviewer may watch mid appeal. Highest severity work, so first in the queue once the verdict lands.

  1. A4-01 Replace or remove the fabricated 5.0 / 43 aggregate. Pull real Google figures once reinstated, or strip the markup until a genuine source exists.
  2. A4-03 · CC-01 · CC-02 Stop showing Cars.com content under Google branding on the hero, the site-wide header, and the Spanish page. Keep English and Spanish in sync.
  3. A4-02 Reconcile the 36 vs 43 count to one sourced figure.
  4. A4-05 Drop Verified wording unless reviews are pulled from a verifying platform.

If counsel prioritizes the FTC fabricated-review exposure, the review cluster can be expedited by removing the aggregate and Google branding, a subtractive de-risk that is safer than restating new numbers mid freeze.
